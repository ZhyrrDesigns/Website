Process

Simple structure, clear checkpoints, and frequent communication.

1
Direction

We confirm size, biome layout, progression, and the world vibe. I suggest what plays best for your player count.

2
Slot secured

Deposit secures your spot. After that, I start the build and set up update checkpoints.

3
Build and feedback

You receive progress screenshots. You can request adjustments while the world is being shaped.

4
Final preview

You receive final screenshots, you approve, then delivery. Small finishing tweaks are included after testing.

If you are not sure about size, tell me your player count and how long you want the world to last. I will recommend the best option.
