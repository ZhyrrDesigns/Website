Commission Pipeline

We plan the world together, I build with frequent updates, and you receive a finished survival-ready map.

1
Idea & Direction

We discuss map size vs. player count, biome spacing, progression, landmarks, and the overall vibe. I’ll give honest suggestions (what plays best) while staying true to your vision.

2
Planning & Downpayment

Once the plan is locked in, a 50% downpayment secures your slot. After that, I start building.

3
Build Phase & Feedback

I send frequent progress screenshots and updates. You can request adjustments while we build, so there are no surprises at the end.

4
Final Preview & Delivery

You receive final in-game screenshots as a preview. After the remaining 50%, I deliver the map and you can request small finishing tweaks after testing.

Tip: if you’re unsure about size/layout, tell me your player count + how long you want the world to last — I’ll help you pick.

Commission FAQ

What do the prices include?

Handcrafted terrain, biome composition and transitions, coastlines/oceans (if requested), and survival-focused world flow. Prices are estimates and may change based on complexity and deadlines.

How long does a commission take?

It depends on map size and complexity. Once you message me with your size and goals, I’ll give you a clear estimate before we start.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes, small finishing tweaks are included after you test the map. Bigger changes can be discussed separately.

How do I get started?

Message me on Discord (Zhyrr) or email info@zhyrrdesigns.com with: preferred map size, expected player count, and the vibe/biomes you want.
