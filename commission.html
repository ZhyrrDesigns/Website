Commission FAQ

What do the prices include?

Handcrafted terrain, biome composition and transitions, and a survival focused layout. Pricing is an estimate and may change based on complexity and deadlines.

How long does a commission take?

It depends on map size and complexity. Message me your size and goals and I will give a clear estimate before we begin.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes. Small finishing tweaks are included after you test the map. Bigger changes can be discussed separately.

How do I get started?

Message me on Discord or email with your size, player count, and the vibe you want. Use the template on this page for the fastest reply.
